    How to Play 📖

    🎭 Multiplayer (2–10 players, one device)

    1. Setup: enter every player's name, pick a word category and a clue timer (15, 30 or 45 seconds).
    2. Secret roles: the game picks one secret word and secretly makes one player the Imposter. Everyone else is a regular player and gets the same word.
    3. Pass the device: each player privately taps Reveal My Role, remembers it, then taps Hide & Pass. The Imposter only sees the category — not the word.
    4. Clue round: one by one, each player says a clue about the word out loud — but never the word itself. The Imposter must bluff! The host can start, pause, resume or skip the timer.
    5. Voting: pass the device again. Each player secretly votes for who they think the Imposter is. You can't vote for yourself, and no votes are shown until everyone has voted.
    6. Result: the player with the most votes is revealed. If they are the Imposter, they get one final guess at the secret word.
    7. Ties: if the vote is tied, everyone revotes, choosing only between the tied players. If it is still tied, nobody is caught and the Imposter escapes.

    ⭐ Multiplayer scoring

    Points are added once per round, to the current game score and to the saved leaderboard.

    💡 Clue tips

    • Be vague enough that the Imposter can't work out the word…
    • …but clear enough that other players know you're not the Imposter.
    • Imposter: listen closely to earlier clues and give something that fits.

    👥 Playing with only 2 players

    It works, but it's mostly a bluffing game: each player knows that if they are not the Imposter, the other person is. Votes will usually tie, so the Imposter often escapes. For the best social-deduction fun, play with 4 or more.

    🔒 About privacy

    SecretWord is a casual pass-the-device game. Roles are hidden on screen, but someone who opens the browser's developer tools could find the word. Play fair and have fun!
